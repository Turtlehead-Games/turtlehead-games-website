DEVELOPMENT NOTES
2026.09.28

From Godot to Unity

We originally started experimenting with Godot using C#, and actually loved it, but eventually decided to switch to Unity as our primary game engine. While we really enjoyed Godot, our ultimate goal is to build a co-op game, and online resources suggested Unity is better suited for our networking needs. The transition has been a solid learning experience. We're getting familiar with Unity's editor, scenes, prefabs, components, scripts, hierarchy, and how all the different systems fit together. For our first project, we're focused on keeping the scope small and actually finishing something rather than getting lost trying to build our dream game right away.
